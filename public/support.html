Frequently asked

Is this financial advice?

No. Apex Wave Capital publishes educational market analysis. It is not personal advice and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

What does “invalidation level” mean?

It is the price at which the published wave count is no longer valid. If the market trades beyond it, the analysis is void and a new count is needed.

What is the live depot?

A simulated depot without real money. Each position shows its buy zone, stop, target and proof. Wins and losses both stay on the record.

How is the hit rate calculated?

Closed calls that reached their target before the stop, divided by all closed calls. The number of closed calls is always shown next to it.
