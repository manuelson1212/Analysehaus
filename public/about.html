About

A research desk for people who want to see the work.

Analysehaus is a focused Elliott Wave publication. We show the count, the logic and the level that would prove us wrong.

Our approach

We follow classical Elliott Wave practice in the tradition of Frost and Prechter: impulses of five waves, corrections of three, and a fixed set of rules that a valid count has to respect.

Waves are labelled on the chart and explained in writing. Where the structure allows more than one reading, we publish the alternate scenario with its own levels.

What we cover

Major cryptocurrencies and equity indices, mostly on four-hour and daily charts, plus single stocks when the structure is clear.

Analyses are published when a setup deserves documenting, not on a fixed schedule.

How we work

Invalidation before targets

Alternates are published

If the chart allows two readings, you see both.

No hype

No guaranteed outcomes, no urgency, no pressure to act.
